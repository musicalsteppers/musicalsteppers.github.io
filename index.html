About Us · 关于我们

传承文化
连接社区

Culture · Heritage · Community

斯岛踏歌行成立于2023年，是位于纽约市史泰登岛的华人文艺表演团体。我们致力于通过歌唱与舞蹈，传承和弘扬中华优秀传统文化。
Founded in 2023, Staten Island Musical Steppers is a Chinese cultural performing arts group based in Staten Island, NYC. We are dedicated to preserving and promoting Chinese cultural heritage through song and dance.

我们的成员来自史泰登岛及周边地区的华人社区，热爱艺术，积极服务社区，致力于促进多元文化交流与融合。
Our members are passionate community artists from Staten Island and surrounding areas, dedicated to multicultural exchange and inclusion.

自成立以来，SIMS积极参与多元文化社区活动，包括里士满县地方检察院多元文化美食节、寄兴园中秋节、NYC DOT地球日及夏日街坊节，并前往养老院和护理院慰问演出，亦应华夏中文学校邀请服务更广大华人社区。
Since its founding, SIMS has actively served multicultural communities, performing at events hosted by the Richmond County District Attorney's Office, NYC DOT, HuaXia Chinese School, senior centers, and nursing homes across Staten Island and beyond.

What We Do · 我们的活动

艺术表演
社区服务

Arts, Culture & Community Programs

💃
中华舞蹈
Traditional Chinese Dance

表演秧歌、九寨、梅花赋、渔光曲、女驸马、草原上的月亮等多种中国民族舞蹈，展现中华文化的优美与多样性。

Performing Yangko, Jiuzhai, Plum Blossom, Fishing Light Song, Lady General, Moon on the Grassland, and more.

🎵
歌唱演出
Vocal Performances

演唱春秋亭、在那桃花盛开的地方、莫斯科郊外的晚上、美丽的草原我的家等经典歌曲，为社区带来美好音乐体验。

Performing beloved Chinese classics including Chunqiu Pavilion, Moscow Nights, Beautiful Grassland My Home, and more.

🏮
节日庆典
Festival Celebrations

在春节、元宵节、中秋节等重要传统节日举办文艺演出，将中华传统节日文化带给纽约市民。

Bringing Chinese festival traditions to NYC during Lunar New Year, Lantern Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, and more.

🤝
社区合作
Community Partnership

与史泰登岛及纽约市各社区组织合作，促进多元文化交流，增进不同族裔之间的了解与友谊。

合作单位包括：里士满县地方检察院、纽约市交通局（NYC DOT）、华夏中文学校、华康会、史岛华人基督教教会等。

Partnering with the Richmond County District Attorney's Office, NYC DOT, HuaXia Chinese School, HuaKang Association, Staten Island Chinese Christian Church, and more.

🎭
文化展示
Cultural Showcases

参与城市文化节、公共艺术活动，向更广泛的纽约市民展示中华文化的独特魅力。

Taking part in city festivals and public arts events, showcasing the unique beauty of Chinese culture to New Yorkers of all backgrounds.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
家庭友好
Family-Friendly Events

所有活动均向公众免费开放，欢迎各族裔家庭参与，共同体验中华文化艺术的魅力。

All events are free and open to the public. Families of all backgrounds are warmly welcomed to experience the beauty of Chinese culture and performing arts.
